CLEAN, SAFE, SCALABLE POWER

[

Unlimited energy

]

Fusion fuel is abundant.

Deuterium is collected from water, and helium-3 is produced through fusion reactions. Unlike fossil fuels, fusion isn’t constrained by scarce resources or geopolitics.

[

Inherently safe

]

No runaway chain reactions, no long-lived waste.

Fusion starts and stops instantly, so it can’t run out of control. It’s carbon-free and does not produce the long-lived radioactive waste associated with traditional nuclear power.

[

always on

]

Consistent energy without compromise.

Fusion plants can operate 24/7, regardless of weather, providing continuous carbon-free power to the grid.
