have you ever wished you could instantly rewind when listening
to the radio to hear the title of the song just played? OB–4 memorizes everything you listen to on an endless looping tape*.             
rewind, time-bend, loop, keep time or zone out, all at the flick of your fingertips. on purpose, or by accident. OB–4 let's you listen like never before, and that's just the beginning of its many magic tricks.
