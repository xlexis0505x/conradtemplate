Janus: The Launch Pad for Commercial Deployments

One product for government and commercial customers. Radiant will deploy 15 reactors to the military by 2030 that are identical to the units we will deliver to our commercial customers.

From prototype to product:

Janus volume establishes Kaleidos as a repeatable energy product.

Capacity built for scale:

Janus accelerates the timeline to mass-produce reactors. Kaleidos will be refined through multiple design iterations incorporating inputs from the government’s top experts.

Real-world operating experience:

A fleet of Kaleidos will be deployed to bases across the US, generating years of combined operating experience each calendar year. This is an exponential accelerant to Radiant’s readiness for deployments at scale.

A proven path to market:

The Janus program shortens the runway to adoption across government, national security, data centers, physical AI, 24/7 critical load, main street businesses and more.

Volume to reduce costs:

The production volume achieved through the Janus program enables the reliability, efficiency and cost reduction required for commercial deployments while reducing costs for government customers.
